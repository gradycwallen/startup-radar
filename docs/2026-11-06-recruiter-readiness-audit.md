## High priority

1. **Signal observation jobs are not completing.** Fifteen most recent scheduled runs, October 23–November 6, 2026, concluded `cancelled`. The [November 6 run](https://github.com/gradycwallen/startup-radar/actions/runs/42284276788) ran from 19:58:22 to 20:13:38 UTC; its collection step was cancelled and the log reported only that the operation was canceled. This aligns with the workflow's `timeout-minutes: 15`, suggesting timeout, but the log does not itself state the cause. Rows written before cancellation remain: latest observations at the audit snapshot were November 6. Fresh rows alone do not establish full coverage or successful job completion. Discovery's November 6 scheduled run succeeded.
2. **Charter Space is linked to another site's domain.** `companies.id=57` stores `space.com` although the company's [own funding announcement](https://www.charter.space/news/seed-round-2026) is hosted at `charter.space`. Active website source id 82 targets `https://space.com/`; at snapshot it has 254 observations and the company has zero signals. Inspect dependencies and correct with a scoped, explicitly authorized database plan; do not bulk-delete observations without a separate review.
3. **Kabilio's funding round is misidentified.** The stored discovery reason describes the €10M round as pre-seed. The [source article](https://www.eu-startups.com/2026/11/barcelonas-kabilio-raises-e10-million-to-deploy-its-ai-accounting-agent-as-client-base-nears-500-firms/) says the round's leaders also led an earlier pre-seed, and does not label this €10M round pre-seed. The stored reason also presents a USD conversion without an explanatory rate or provenance.
4. **Biograil's round is misidentified.** Its stored reason calls the €20M financing seed. The [company announcement](https://biograil.com/2026/11/04/press-release-biograil-raises-eur-20-million-financing-round-to-advance-preparations-forclinical-trials/) says HTGF led a seed syndicate in 2019 and participated in this round through a growth fund; it does not call the new round seed.
5. **XDOF funding display may overstate the reporting.** Its source [reports Series B talks](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/04/xdof-just-three-months-out-of-stealth-is-in-talks-for-a-series-b-at-a-1-2b-valuation/), not a completed Series B. The stored discovery reason distinguishes these talks from an earlier completed Series A, but the generic Funding label and synthetic headline do not make the distinction as clear.

## Frontend and repository

- Company profile observation history is paginated, but `frontend/src/lib/data/companies.ts` still reads companies, discovery events and summary signals without pagination. At this snapshot these tables contain 238, 653 and 97 rows respectively, below the usual 1,000-row cap; this is a future truncation risk rather than a proven current omission.
- A local 390px-wide view rendered the homepage and a Restate profile. On a long signal history, the research brief follows the full signal section on mobile, so it is hard to reach. Search by GitHits returned one matching company. This was not a full clean-browser, keyboard, offline, or deployed-site audit.
- Removing the theme switch leaves stored or OS-selected dark mode in effect. A visitor cannot choose a theme within the site; this is current behavior, not evidence that the theme fails.
- The README and Methodology make unconditional claims about every claim/event having evidence or a link. The verified Charter Space mismatch demonstrates why these claims should be qualified. README's screenshot predates the current redesign. `frontend/README.md` says the frontend reads only companies and discovery events, but it also reads signal tables.
- `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` is read in a server-only module; no tracked `.env` or `.mcp.json` appeared in a tracked-file name check. This is not a full secret scan of Git history or a permissions audit of the database.
